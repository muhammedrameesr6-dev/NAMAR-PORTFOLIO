I’m a marketing professional who started my journey with an interest in understanding how brands grow, connect with people, and turn attention into results.

My professional journey started in May 2025 at Futura Labs, where I completed my Digital Marketing course and later joined the company as an intern for 2 months. Working on real projects helped me understand how digital marketing works beyond theory.

That internship made me realise that marketing was the right field for me. I enjoyed the creative side, understanding audiences, building content ideas, and thinking about why people connect with certain brands.

After completing my internship, I focused on getting into a good agency where I could learn and work with different brands. In my first attempt, I got the opportunity to join Desgro Media as a Social Media Manager. I worked at Desgro Media for one year, until October 4, and this became one of the most important learning experiences in my career.

Working with different businesses taught me that every brand needs a different approach. I worked on social media management, content strategy, production, personal branding, company branding, and marketing strategy for different clients.
